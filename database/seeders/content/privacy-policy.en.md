# Privacy Policy for ADD (Aleppo Digital District) App

**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to the **ADD (Aleppo Digital District)** application. At ADD Coworking Space, we are committed to safeguarding the privacy and security of our members and visitors. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and disclose your personal data when you access our mobile and web applications to reserve desks, private offices, meeting rooms, or participate in our digital innovation community.

## 2. Information We Collect
We collect information necessary to provide an optimal workspace environment, including:
* **Account & Profile Information:** Full name, email address, phone number, company/organization name, and job title.
* **Reservation & Usage Details:** Booking history for open desks, dedicated spaces, conference rooms, event tickets, and community workshop attendance.
* **Access Control & Network Logs:** Smart digital door access logs within ADD premises, keyless entry records, and authentication logs on the ADD Wi-Fi network.
* **Financial & Billing Data:** Subscription tiers, transaction history, and invoice records (all payments are handled via secure third-party payment gateways; we do not store full payment card details).
* **Technical Diagnostics:** Device identifiers, IP addresses, operating system version, and application performance crash logs to maintain software stability.

## 3. How We Use Your Information
We process your personal information for the following operational purposes:
1. To facilitate membership subscriptions and manage desk or room reservations.
2. To authorize digital key access and maintain the physical security of ADD premises.
3. To process payments, issue formal invoices, and manage membership renewals.
4. To communicate important updates, booking confirmations, maintenance notices, and community event schedules.
5. To monitor and optimize workspace connectivity, bandwidth allocation, and overall app performance.
6. To satisfy applicable legal and regulatory requirements and protect the integrity of our community.

## 4. Data Sharing and Third Parties
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Data is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis with:
* **Cloud & Infrastructure Providers:** Trusted enterprise cloud hosting and database services operating with robust encryption.
* **Payment Processors:** Certified financial transaction platforms that execute secure membership billing.
* **Smart Access Vendors:** Digital security and IoT hardware management systems that control physical access to the building and offices.
* **Legal Authorities:** Regulatory or law enforcement entities when mandatory under applicable laws or official orders.

## 5. Data Security and Retention
We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical controls—including end-to-end SSL/TLS encryption, restricted access protocols, and proactive firewall protection—to protect against unauthorized access, destruction, or disclosure. We retain your information for as long as your account remains active or as required by law for accounting and auditing purposes.

## 6. Your Rights
As a registered user of ADD, you have the right to:
* Access and review the personal information we hold about you.
* Correct or update any inaccurate or incomplete personal records via your profile settings.
* Request the complete erasure of your profile and data, subject to legal compliance requirements.
* Opt out of community newsletters, notifications, or promotional messages at any time.

## 7. Cookies and Local Storage
Our application utilizes local cache and session tokens to preserve authentication sessions, remember user preferences, and guarantee responsive navigation. You can manage local storage permissions through your device settings.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may periodically update this policy to reflect platform improvements, operational changes, or new legal frameworks. Material modifications will be communicated via in-app alerts or registered email notifications prior to becoming effective.
