# Help & Support

We're here to help you get the most out of **ADD (Aleppo Digital District)**. Find quick answers below, or reach our team through any of the contact options on this page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I book a space?
Open **Spaces**, choose a room, desk or office, pick a date and the time slots you need, then confirm and choose how to pay. Some spaces need our team's approval before the booking is confirmed.

### How do I cancel a booking?
Open the booking from **My Bookings** and tap **Cancel**. A booking can be cancelled only within the cancellation window shown when you made it.

### How do I get in?
Once your booking is confirmed, open the door with the QR in the app, or use the door code shown in your booking. In a co-working space, scan the kiosk QR at reception to start your session.

### How do I pay?
From your wallet, in cash at reception, or by card in the app — whichever is offered for that purchase. You can top up your wallet at reception or in the app.

### I forgot my password
On the sign-in screen tap **Forgot password**, enter your phone number, and use the code we send you on WhatsApp to set a new one.

### How do I end my session?
Tap **End session** in the app and choose how to pay, or check out at reception.
